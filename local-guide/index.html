Why is Jackson, GA so popular?

Jackson, Georgia is popularized because Stranger Things, the Netflix series was filmed in Jackson. The Square is filled with Stranger Things related places and activities for fans like coffe shops, clothing stores, escape rooms, tours, and more.

Stranger Things fans visiting Downtown Jackson, Georgia will instantly recognize the town square as the real-life setting for fictional Hawkins, Indiana. As you stroll around the square, you can see filming locations such as the courthouse that appeared as the Hawkins Public Library, the theater featured in the series, and the alley made famous by memorable scenes from the show. Fans can also enjoy Stranger Things-themed murals, photo opportunities, Hawkins Headquarters merchandise shop, guided filming-location tours, and even an escape room inspired by the Upside Down. With its preserved small-town charm and recognizable landmarks, Downtown Jackson offers visitors the chance to step directly into the world of Stranger Things and experience the magic of Hawkins for themselves.
